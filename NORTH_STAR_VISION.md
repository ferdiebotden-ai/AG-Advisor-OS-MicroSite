## What We're Building

AgAdvisor OS is a **voice-first, AI-native operating system** purpose-built for Canadian agriculture advisors. It's not another generic CRM—it's a specialized system that understands agriculture workflows, Canadian compliance requirements, and the unique relationship-driven nature of agricultural finance.

### The Core Concept: "AI Drafts, Humans Approve"

Every interaction follows the same pattern:

```
CAPTURE → PROCESS → DRAFT → APPROVE → EXECUTE → AUDIT
   ↑         ↑         ↑        ↑          ↑        ↑
 Voice      AI      AI       Human      System   Logged
```

The AI does the heavy lifting. The human maintains control. Nothing becomes "truth" without explicit approval.

### Three Integrated Modules

**Module 1: Voice-First CRM**
The foundation. Every client conversation—phone call, field visit, voice memo—becomes a structured interaction record. AI drafts the call report, extracts action items, suggests CRM updates, and queues follow-up communications. The advisor reviews, edits if needed, and approves with one click.

**Module 2: Farm Appraisal Pro**
Specialized farmland valuation workflow. Spatial search across Ontario parcel data, AI-assisted comparable analysis, and CUSPAP-compliant report generation. The appraiser applies professional judgment, the system handles data gathering and formatting.

**Module 3: Agriculture Credit Analyst**
Financial statement analysis built for agriculture. Understands seasonal cash flows, commodity cycles, and production metrics. Generates draft credit memos with all supporting ratios—but the credit decision is always human.

### How They Connect

The three modules share a unified client record. When an advisor captures a call about a refinancing discussion, that context flows to the appraisal workflow. When the appraisal is complete, the asset values feed into credit analysis. The advisor sees one complete client picture—not three siloed tools.

## Core Principles

### 1. Human-in-the-Loop is Non-Negotiable

AI drafts. Humans approve. This isn't a limitation—it's the entire point.

Agriculture advisory involves judgment, relationships, and regulatory responsibility. AI should augment human capability, not replace human accountability. Every AI output includes:
- Clear indication it's a draft
- Link back to source evidence
- Explicit approval action required
- Complete audit trail of changes

### 2. Compliance is a Feature, Not a Constraint

Canadian financial services operate under PIPEDA, provincial regulations, and institutional policies. We don't bolt compliance on—we build it in:

**Voice Recording Compliance:**
- One-party consent jurisdiction (Ontario), but we exceed minimum requirements
- Clear consent capture mechanisms
- Opt-out alternatives for objecting parties
- Retention controls and deletion workflows

**Audit Trail:**
- Every interaction versioned and timestamped
- Every AI draft preserved alongside human edits
- Every approval logged with user identity
- Every access recorded

**Data Residency:**
- Canadian infrastructure (AWS ca-central-1)
- No data leaves the country
- PIPEDA-compliant processing

### 3. Voice-First, Not Voice-Only

Voice is the natural interface for busy advisors—they're driving between farms, walking fields, finishing meetings. But voice-first means:
- Voice capture as primary input
- Structured data as output
- Traditional interfaces available when needed
- Mobile-native design

### 4. Agriculture-Native Intelligence

Generic CRMs don't understand:
- Seasonal cash flows (plant in spring, harvest in fall, income in winter)
- Commodity price cycles and their impact on collateral values
- Production metrics by commodity type
- Canadian regulatory specifics (CUSPAP for appraisals, FCC guidelines)

We build agriculture understanding into every layer—vocabulary, workflows, analytics.

### 5. Evidence-Based Transparency

Every AI output shows its work:
- Summary statements link to transcript excerpts
- Extracted entities show source context
- Suggested values show comparable data
- Confidence levels displayed where relevant

Advisors can trust the system because they can verify it.
