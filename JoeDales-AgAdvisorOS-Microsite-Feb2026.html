Transform every client conversation into structured, compliant interaction records. The AI drafts call reports, suggests CRM updates, and creates tasks—but nothing becomes truth until you approve it.

  • AI-generated call reports with source evidence
  • Suggested CRM updates (contacts, opportunities, tasks)
